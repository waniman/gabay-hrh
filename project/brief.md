# Project brief

Approved by the lead: 2026-11-16 (EM-001, D-004)

This brief covers the project as a whole. Each work package has its own brief in `project/wp/<WPn-name>/brief.md`, and the results chain is in `project/results.md`. Facts below come from the files listed in `project/sources/index.md`. Each data gap is stated where the missing fact would be used.

## Purpose

Gabay HRH (Governance Assessment Building and Advancing Your LGU's Human Resources for Health) is the work of the ASMPH Center for Research and Innovation (ACRI) on health workforce planning and investment at the subnational level (URC proposal, 2026).

Local government units (LGUs) hold the authority to create health positions under the Local Government Code of 1991 (Republic Act No. 7160). They decide without a quantified staffing requirement, and the path from a staffing gap to a funded plantilla position is not documented (URC proposal, 2026). The people affected are the health workers who carry the workload and the communities their facilities serve.

The project has two tracks.

| Track | What it is | Status | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gabay HRH pilot | A staffing needs (WISN) and governance assessment in the Municipality of Paracelis, Mountain Province, over 12 months, with a budget of PHP 599,850.00 | Funded by the URC grant, for implementation in 2027 (lead, 2026-11-16) | URC proposal, 2026 |
| GabayHRH 2.0 | An 18-month expansion to five LGU sites, December 2026 to May 2028 | Proposal in draft; budget incomplete; no funder named | 2.0 draft, 2026-11-16 |

## Audience

- **Local chief executives, local health boards, municipal and provincial health officers, and local finance committees.** They should know the staffing requirement of their facilities and act on it in the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH) and the budget (URC proposal, 2026).
- **The Department of Health (DOH) Health Human Resource Development Bureau (HHRDB).** It should assess and use the guide for LGU health workforce planning and investment (URC proposal, 2026).
- **A funder for GabayHRH 2.0.** It should decide to fund the multi-site study. The 2.0 draft names no funder.
- **Researchers and practitioners in health workforce governance.** They are the readers of the journal article (URC proposal, 2026).

## Terms for results

The team uses outputs, outcomes, impact and assumptions (`core/results-chain.md`). The URC proposal uses "expected outputs" and "potential impact"; these map to outputs and impact. The 2.0 draft uses "deliverable output" and "policy and system impact"; the second maps to outcomes.

## Limits

- Budget ceiling: PHP 599,850.00 for the pilot (URC proposal, 2026). No ceiling is recorded for GabayHRH 2.0.
- Final deadline: not recorded for either track. The pilot runs four quarters (URC proposal, 2026). The 2.0 draft ends in May 2028 and marks its dates tentative (2.0 draft, 2026-11-16).
- Limits on the approach:
  - No data collection before ethics clearance and a signed Memorandum of Understanding with the LGU (URC proposal, 2026; Concept note, 2026).
  - No finding leaves the team before it is presented to the LGU it concerns (D-005). Findings of the URC pilot go first to the Municipality of Paracelis, as the URC proposal commits (URC proposal, 2026). Findings of GabayHRH 2.0 go first to its partner LGUs. This binds the article, the policy brief, the validation workshop and any use of pilot findings in the 2.0 proposal.
  - No data collection at a GabayHRH 2.0 site before an ethics clearance that names that site.
  - Raw data that can identify a person never enter this repository. Only de-identified, derived files are committed.
  - No new analysis or statistical test runs before the lead approves it (`core/methods-rules.md`).

## AI use

Proposed for the lead's decision at the frame gate (compliance check, item 5.4).

- AI agents draft and check the outputs. The lead and the named authors direct the argument, the interpretation and the conclusions, and approve every version that leaves the team.
- Each work package keeps an `ai-use.md` from its brief stage, recording what the agents did.
- Each output follows the AI policy of its recipient: the funder's for the proposal, the journal's and its publisher's for the article. The policies of the URC grant and of the university are not on file.
- Study data that can identify a person are not given to AI tools.

## Must include

- A comparison with the guide for LGUs on health workforce planning that the DOH has already developed, which the lead reported at the frame gate (D-006). The guide and the outputs that propose a new guide (WP1, WP3) state what the DOH guide covers and what the project adds. Its title, issuer and scope enter the text only once verified in `project/wp/WP1-gabayhrh-2-0-proposal/work/evidence.md`.
- The WHO Workload Indicators of Staffing Need (WISN) method for staffing requirements (URC proposal, 2026).
- The governance analysis of how staffing evidence becomes, or does not become, a funded position (URC proposal, 2026).
- The four conditioning variables carried into each decision point of the guide: income classification, geographically isolated and disadvantaged area status, headroom under the personal services limitation, and local health system integration under the Universal Health Care Act (Republic Act No. 11223) (Response to RCWI comments, 2026).

## Must avoid

- A claim that a framework derived from one site is replicable. The response to the reviewers removed this claim; findings extend to a defined class of LGUs (Response to RCWI comments, 2026).
- Naming an LGU as a partner or a site before it signs a letter of intent or a Memorandum of Understanding. The 2.0 draft records talks, not commitments (2.0 draft, 2026-11-16).
- A claim that the project caused an outcome or an impact. The project contributes.
