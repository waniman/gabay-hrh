# Results chain

Approved by the lead: not yet

One page. It states what the project delivers, the outcomes it aims to influence, the impact it contributes to, and the assumptions outside its control. Terms and rules: `core/results-chain.md`. The lead approves it at the project frame gate. A change to it afterwards changes scope and goes to the lead.

## Impact

One sentence: whose conditions or well-being should change, and in what way. The project contributes to this change with others. The team states the contribution and makes no claim that the project caused it.

## Outcomes

Two or three. Each names who should know, decide or do what. Write each on one line, in this form, so the tool can read it: `- O1. <who> <knows, decides or does what>. By: YYYY-MM-DD`

## Outputs

One line for each output: the output, its work package, and the outcome it serves. For example: "Concept note (WP2), serves O1."

## Assumptions

The conditions outside the project that must hold for the outputs to lead to the outcomes, and for the outcomes to contribute to the impact. Number them A1, A2 and so on. An assumption that fails is reported to the lead in a memo.

## Signals

For each outcome, what would show that it happened, and where the lead, a partner or the team would see it. Observed signals are logged with `python3 tools/team.py outcome add`.
