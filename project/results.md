# Results chain

Approved by the lead: not yet

One page. Terms and rules: `core/results-chain.md`. Sources are listed in `project/sources/index.md`. The dates are proposed for the lead's approval; the note under each outcome states the basis.

## Impact

People served by the partner LGUs' primary care facilities, including people in geographically isolated and disadvantaged areas, receive care from facilities staffed according to measured workload. The project contributes to this change with the LGUs, the DOH and others; it makes no claim of attribution.

## Outcomes

- O1. A funder commits funding for the GabayHRH 2.0 multi-site study. By: 2027-03-31
- O2. The Municipality of Paracelis uses the diagnostic profile's staffing estimates in its Local Investment Plan for Health and budget proposal. By: 2027-12-31
- O3. Health officers of LGUs outside Paracelis and the DOH HHRDB use the guide and the published findings in health workforce planning. By: 2028-06-30

Basis of the dates:

- O1. The 2.0 draft starts in December 2026 or January 2027 and marks the date tentative (2.0 draft, 2026-11-16). No funder deadline is recorded.
- O2. The pilot's outputs are inputs to the municipality's 2027 LIPH, with implementation in 2028 (URC proposal, 2026). The concept note sets turnover of the profile in September 2027 (Concept note, 2026).
- O3. The guide is validated and released in the fourth quarter of the pilot (URC proposal, 2026). The pilot's start date is not recorded, so the date assumes a January 2027 start and six months for uptake.

## Outputs

- GabayHRH 2.0 proposal (WP1), serves O1.
- LGU HRH diagnostic profile of Paracelis (WP2, planned), serves O2.
- Guide for LGU health workforce planning and investment (WP3, planned), serves O3.
- Journal article (WP4, planned), serves O3.
- Policy brief for local chief executives and local health boards (WP5, planned), serves O2 and O3.

## Assumptions

- A1. The URC grant is released and the ethics committee clears the study before data collection starts.
- A2. The Municipality of Paracelis signs the Memorandum of Understanding and gives access to records and staff time.
- A3. Staffing and service delivery records exist, or observation can substitute for them.
- A4. The profile reaches the LGU before the LIPH and budget calendar closes for the year.
- A5. A funder for GabayHRH 2.0 is identified, and five LGUs commit as sites.
- A6. The DOH HHRDB and LGUs outside the study site take part in the validation workshop.

## Signals

- O1. An award notice or agreement from a funder; signed letters of intent or Memoranda of Understanding from the 2.0 sites. Seen by the lead.
- O2. Staffing estimates cited in the Paracelis LIPH, the Annual Investment Program, local health board minutes, or an ordinance that creates or funds a position. Seen by the lead or the LGU focal persons.
- O3. The validation workshop report; requests for the guide from LGUs or the HHRDB; the journal's decision letter; a DOH or LGU document that cites the guide. Seen by the lead or partners.

Observed signals are logged with `python3 tools/team.py outcome add`.
