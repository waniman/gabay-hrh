# Notes on the URC proposal

Key facts taken from "[CLEANED] FINAL Proposal, LGU HRH Needs Assessment.docx" (Drive id `1vN6DThdCd79Yxxn2VDamBSHKuufZOtxz`, modified 2026-08-31), read through the Drive connector on 2026-11-16. These notes are not the source. Check the file before a fact enters an output.

- **Title.** Governance Assessment Building and Advancing Your LGU's Human Resources for Health (Gabay HRH).
- **Proponents.** Theo Prudencio Juhani Z. Capeding, Head, Health Governance Flagship, ASMPH Center for Research and Innovation (ACRI); Hazel Ivy M. Jeremias, Research Associate, Health Governance Flagship, ACRI.
- **Objectives.**
  1. Determine staffing requirements and workload pressure across priority cadres and facilities in the partner municipality, using the WHO Workload Indicators of Staffing Need (WISN) method.
  2. Identify the administrative, fiscal and institutional constraints that prevent identified staffing gaps from becoming funded positions.
  3. Develop and validate a guide for local health workforce planning and investment.
- **Site.** Municipality of Paracelis, Mountain Province: a second class municipality and a geographically isolated and disadvantaged area. A letter of intent was secured.
- **Design.** Case study, mixed-methods explanatory design. Facilities are the units of analysis, with priority cadres within them.
- **Framework.** Principal-agent framework (Brinkerhoff and Bossert, 2014), at two levels: DOH provincial and regional offices with the LGU, and the LGU with providers.
- **Phases.** 1, inception and preparation (quarter 1); 2, data collection (quarters 2 and 3); 3, analysis (quarters 2 to 4); 4, outputs, validation and dissemination (quarters 2 to 4).
- **Outputs.**
  - One peer-reviewed journal article, in Human Resources for Health
  - One policy brief for local chief executives and local health boards
  - One LGU HRH diagnostic profile for the partner LGU, formatted as input to the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH)
  - One validated guide for LGU health workforce planning and investment for low-resource settings
- **Use.** The outputs are inputs to the municipality's 2027 LIPH, with implementation in 2028.
- **Ethics.** Clearance from the University Research Ethics Committee before any data collection; written informed consent; findings reported in a form that does not identify individual health workers; findings presented to the LGU before external dissemination.
- **Budget.** PHP 599,850.00 in total, as proposed. The amount awarded is not on file.
  - Salaries and wages: Project Lead PHP 120,000.00; Research Associate PHP 180,000.00; Research Assistant PHP 75,000.00
  - Honoraria and services: expert working group members PHP 64,000.00; transcriber PHP 22,500.00
  - Travel: daily subsistence allowance PHP 52,500.00; transportation PHP 28,350.00
  - Supplies and materials: Zoom subscription PHP 7,500.00; representation PHP 28,000.00; communications allowance for online interview participants PHP 10,000.00; communications allowance for project staff PHP 12,000.00
  - The table has no line for publication costs.
- **Review round.** Four reviewer comments were answered on 31 August 2026: LGU endorsement; sufficiency of a single case; contextual differences among LGUs; workplan alignment with the LGU's schedule (Response to RCWI Comments, Drive id `1Hme5-aCpJshzkEgbKAvLS_M3BXBsnqj0jRRWNQtvZ2g`).
