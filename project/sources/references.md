## DOH guidance for LGUs on health workforce planning

**Department of Health. (2022).** Minimum recommendations for staffing complement of public health facilities and health offices in local government units (Annex C-2). In *Devolution transition plan 2022–2024* (updated January 20, 2022). Health Policy Development and Planning Bureau. https://galingpook.org/documents/64/Joint-DOH-NNC-DTP-Narrative-and-Annexes_20Jan2022_signed.pdf

- Findings E-007 to E-013, verified. Written by the BLHSD, HFDB and HHRDB; approved by the DBM on 24 March 2022 (E-007). It gives "DOH's guidance to LGUs" on the minimum staffing complement, and LGUs are "highly encouraged" to adopt it (E-008). It uses population ratios "pending the results of the" WISN study (E-011), and phases position creation over five years with a rating rubric (E-012).
- The text layer of Annex C-2 does not mention the personal services limitation, an ordinance, income class, the budget calendar or plantilla (E-013). Twelve image-only organization charts were not checked.
- Its status after Executive Order No. 103, s. 2025 is not confirmed; that order requires new transition plans (E-014).

**Department of Health. (2026).** *Health and care workforce challenges in a devolved health system: Philippine HRH landscape, 2025 snapshot* [Conference presentation slides]. PMAC 2026. https://pmac-2026.com/local/storage/uploads/sessionMaterial/Kenneth%20Ronquillo-20260225-282248.pdf

- Finding E-001, verified. Slide 14 shows the cover of a "Health Workforce Planning Tool Kit" under "KRA 5: NHRHMP Institutionalization and Localization". The slide does not name the tool kit's publisher, and the tool kit itself is not yet citable (see the last section).
