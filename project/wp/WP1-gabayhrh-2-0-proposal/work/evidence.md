## Scan 1: DOH guides for LGUs on health workforce planning (16 November 2026)

**Question from the lead.** The DOH has already developed a guide for LGUs on HRH planning or management. Find it and record it.

**Answer, first pass.** There are two DOH products addressed to LGUs on HRH planning. A third group of DOH issuances sets the frame they sit in.

1. **The DOH "Health Workforce Planning Tool Kit" (E-001 to E-006).** This is most likely the guide the lead means. A DOH slide deck from February 2026 shows its cover under "NHRHMP Institutionalization and Localization". The official copy was not reached. Two parts were seen only as user uploads on Scribd:
   - an "LGU HRH Plan Outline" with the footer "NHRHMP Toolkit";
   - a "Health Workforce Assessment, Monitoring, and Evaluation Tool", Version 2, April 2025.
   
   Neither upload names its issuing office. No DOH issuance that adopts the tool kit was found.
2. **"Minimum Recommendations for Staffing Complement of Public Health Facilities and Health Offices in Local Government Units" (E-007 to E-016).** This is Annex C-2 of the DOH Devolution Transition Plan 2022–2024 and is dated 20 January 2022. It states that it gives "DOH's guidance to LGUs" on staffing. It is advisory. The copy seen is complete, and it is the strongest source to cite.
3. **The frame (E-017 to E-033).** The National HRH Master Plan and the issuances that adopt it; the UHC Act implementing rules; the DOH orders on P/CWHS integration, maturity levels and the Local Investment Plan for Health (LIPH); and DOH technical assistance aids.

**Gap the project can position against.** No DOH document found sets out the steps that turn a staffing gap into a funded plantilla position. None of them deals with the personal services (PS) limitation, the creating ordinance or the local budget calendar:
- The tool kit stops at the gap, the LIPH and lobbying for plantilla positions (E-004).
- Annex C-2 stops at phased priorities and position classification (E-012, E-013).
- The Master Plan names the PS limitation as a barrier but gives no procedure (E-019, E-024).
- AO 2020-0037 names an ordinance-created plantilla only as a maturity milestone (E-026).
